# Home&Sky Hub: privacy policy

_Last updated: 5 November 2026_

Home&Sky Hub is an app that turns an iPad into a display for home and the sky above it. This policy covers the iPad app. It's short because the app collects nothing.

## What the app collects

**Nothing.** The app has no accounts, no servers of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer never receives any of your data.

## What stays on your iPad

Everything you set up stays on the iPad and is deleted when you delete the app:

- your settings (which screens, places, clock style and so on);
- the calendars and reminder lists you choose to show, read from the iPad when the Home screen needs them;
- photos you choose to show, read from the album you pick in Photos (or copied at screen size from Google Photos, if you pick them there);
- your Google sign-in, if you connect Google, kept in the iPad's Keychain.

## Who the app talks to

To show its screens, the app asks these services directly, from your iPad. Each one sees your iPad's internet address, as any website you visit does.

| Service | Why | What it's sent |
|---|---|---|
| adsb.lol, adsbdb, airport-data.com, pics.avs.io | Aircraft nearby, their routes, photos and airline logos | Your location rounded to about 1 km; aircraft codes |
| Open-Meteo | Weather, air quality, tides, place search | Your location rounded to about 1 km; place names you search for |
| RainViewer, OpenStreetMap (or the map provider shown on screen), EOX, NASA GIBS | Rain radar and maps | The map tiles around your rounded location |
| NOAA SWPC, CelesTrak, the people-in-space list | Aurora forecast, the Space Station's orbit and crew | Nothing about you |
| Wikipedia | Article of the day, aircraft types | Nothing about you |
| Google (only if you connect it) | Your calendars, tasks and the photos you pick | Read-only requests made with your sign-in. Google's own privacy policy applies |
| A calendar link you paste (only if you add one) | Your events | A request for that calendar |

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to show your calendars, tasks and chosen photos on your own display. It is never transferred, sold, or used for advertising, and no person reads it.

## Permissions

The app asks iOS for permission before it uses:

- **Location**, while the app is open, to centre the weather and the sky screens. It's rounded to about 1 km before use.
- **Calendars and Reminders**, to show the ones you choose. Read only.
- **Photos**, to show the album you choose. Read only.

You can change or withdraw any of these at any time in the iPad's Settings › Home&Sky Hub.

## Children

The app isn't aimed at children and collects no data from anyone.
